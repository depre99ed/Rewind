Kill sounds, animated images and background removal in the Studio.

- Sound on every kill (Effects tab › On every kill): hitmarker, headshot ding, deep boom, snap, laser, whoosh, bell, or your own sound. Placed right on each detected kill, with the other effects, adjustable volume. The built-in sounds are made by Rewind (no rights to worry about).
- Animated images without keyframes: appear (fade, pop, zoom, spin, slide, drop), loop (spin either way, pulse, float, swing, wiggle, shake) and disappear. Also works for GIFs and videos placed on top.
- Remove an image's background in one click (subject kept, transparent background). Everything runs on your PC; the first time, Rewind downloads the background-removal tool (178 MB, verified).
- Sturdier export with long speed curves and edits full of zoom punches.
