More reliable everywhere: recording, audio, CS2 clips, library and Studio.

- Memory: the RAM buffer can no longer fill the PC, even with a long buffer; past a cap, the oldest parts move to disk without disturbing recording.
- Audio: the “Game” / “Other PC audio” split also works with a manually chosen output and several apps on their own track; nothing is lost or heard twice.
- CS2: the one tap that ends the round is now clipped, and no longer shows twice on the timeline.
- Clips: the notification arrives once the clip is in the library, flags an incomplete clip, and on-screen keys stay in sync after a capture restart.
- “Shorten” replaces the clip in one step (the original still goes to the Recycle Bin); Discord, receiving friends' clips and updates are safer.
- Library: every folder is scanned, and two simultaneous launches no longer damage the index.
- Studio: an emptied project is saved properly, opening is lighter and the preview does no work in the background while stopped.
