A simpler Studio for editing, and Rewind ready for Windows 10 and AMD graphics cards.

- Trimming a clip in the Studio: the edge follows the mouse (even on slow motion), the preview shows the cut frame, effects of a removed kill go with it, and a whole gesture undoes with one Ctrl+Z.
- New: “Keep the kills” trims every clip around its kills in one click; Q and W remove the start or end up to the playhead.
- Bigger Studio: left bar reduced to icons, taller timeline with readable thumbnails, right panel only when something is selected (“Edit” button for the format).
- Windows 10 and AMD: an encoder that refuses the capture frames no longer crashes at every launch (the working mode is remembered), 143.98 Hz screens no longer record at 143 frames per second, Rewind starts even on an older Windows 10 or without the Visual C++ runtime, no more white border around the window.
