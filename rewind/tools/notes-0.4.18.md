The default clip hotkey is now F8, and the player stays in the category you are browsing.

- New installs: F8 keeps the last 30 seconds (it used to be Alt + F10). Your current hotkeys stay the same.
- Player: "Next / previous clip" stays within what is shown (Manual, Edits, a collection, a game, a search), in on-screen order. The filter name appears next to the counter.
- After the opening animation, Rewind always lands on the Clips page.
- Intro ending reworked: the dust reforms the small W in place of the logo, which lands with a short flash; "rewind" lands exactly on the name in the title bar.
- On opening, the sidebar's glass lens drops onto the active tab, then a glint sweeps over it and the Clip button (about 1 s).
- Small animations: the next video slides in from the side you are going, the "Featured" clip changes with a fade.
