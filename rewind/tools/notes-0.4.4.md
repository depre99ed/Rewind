Polished in-game notification, two small fixes.

- The dark rectangle that showed around the in-game notification is gone: only the rounded capsule is displayed.
- The notification now animates as it appears: it rises slightly and settles, then leaves smoothly.
- "Saving…" fades into "Clip saved" instead of switching abruptly; the thumbnail settles in and a thin line shows the time left before the notification disappears.
- Game launch banner: the "live" dot pulses a few times.
- English interface: a clip taken on the desktop is now called "Desktop" (no longer "Bureau").
- Shortcut logo update: other programs' shortcuts are now only opened for reading (no more "access denied" warnings in the log).
