Much faster Studio export, speed curves, and several Studio fixes.

- New: speed curve on each piece (Inspector › Speed). Slow-mo that smoothly speeds back up, speed-up that brakes, a dip in the middle… or your own curve: drag the points, click to add, double-click to remove. Texts and effects follow the picture.
- Export up to 40 times faster when the edit has slow-mo, shakes or zoom punches (a 25 s edit: 7 s instead of 5 min). The result is the same, frame for frame.
- Speed on kill: besides the mini slow-mo, you can now speed up at each kill.
- Kill effects now start 0.2 s earlier by default, to land right on the kill (the kill feed shows up slightly after).
- "Blurred picture" now blurs the whole picture in the preview, edges included (before: a blurred oval in the middle, sharp edges).
- Trimming in the Studio: the timeline no longer rescales while you shorten a piece, nothing moves under the mouse. "Fit all" resets the scale to the edit.
- New: fade to black at the end of the edit (Project panel), picture and sound fade out smoothly, adjustable length.
- Smoother preview during slow-mo (fewer jumps in the video).
